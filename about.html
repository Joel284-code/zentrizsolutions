Our Story

Born in Kenya, Built for Kenya

Zentrix Solutions was founded in 2024 with a clear vision: to make professional web and IT services accessible to every Kenyan business, regardless of size or budget. We saw too many great businesses being left behind online simply because quality digital services were out of reach.

In just two years, we have partnered with over 30 businesses across Kenya — from small shops in Kaimosi to schools in Kisumu and clinics in Kakamega — helping them build professional websites, rank on Google, and make the most of their technology investments.

Today, Zentrix Solutions is proudly headquartered in Kaimosi along the Chvakali–Kapsabet Road, and our team is driven by one simple goal: help every Kenyan business succeed online.
