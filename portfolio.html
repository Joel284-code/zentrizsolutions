Case Study

Wanjiru Medical Clinic: More Patients Through Better Online Presence

Wanjiru Medical Clinic had no online presence and was relying entirely on word of mouth. Zentrix Solutions built a professional clinic website with an appointment booking system and then optimised it for local Google searches in Kakamega.

Within three months of going live, the clinic was ranking on page one for key health searches in the region. Online appointment bookings became a significant part of their new patient flow.
